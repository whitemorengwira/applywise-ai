## 8. DO NOT OVERMATCH

Reject or downgrade a role if the employer requires substantial experience in technologies that the CV does not demonstrate.

Examples:
- Do not claim Java experience.
- Do not claim C#/.NET experience.
- Do not claim SAP experience.
- Do not claim Salesforce experience.
- Do not claim deep MLOps infrastructure experience merely because LLMOps is listed.
- Do not claim data-science/statistical modelling expertise without evidence.
- Do not claim years of experience with a technology merely because it appears in the skills list.
- Do not invent certifications.
- Do not invent degrees.
- Do not invent employment history.

Distinguish:
**demonstrated experience** vs **transferable capability** vs **job requirement not demonstrated**.
