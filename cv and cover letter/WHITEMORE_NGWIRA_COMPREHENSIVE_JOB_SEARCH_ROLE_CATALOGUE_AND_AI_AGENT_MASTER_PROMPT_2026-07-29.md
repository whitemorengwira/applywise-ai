## 6. Title collisions and automatic exclusions

Search terms can be misleading. Apply these exclusions:

- “Architect” must mean systems, solutions, software, cloud, platform, application, enterprise, data/integration or digital architecture. Exclude registered building, interior and landscape architecture.
- “Automation Engineer” must mean AI, workflow, business-process, CRM, marketing or software automation. Exclude PLC, SCADA, industrial, electrical and mechanical automation.
- “Project Manager” may cover AI, software, digital, creative, media, marketing, SaaS, implementation, transformation, operations, general business, NGO, education or public-sector programmes when the core work is coordination, delivery, stakeholder management and reporting. Exclude construction, civil works, clinical trials and specialist finance when a specialist sector qualification or regulated background is mandatory.
- “Product Manager” should focus on SaaS, platforms, software, digital products or AI. Exclude highly specialised physical, pharmaceutical or clinical products.
- “Account Manager” may be suitable; “Accountant”, accounts payable, audit and bookkeeping are not.
- Exclude accountant, chartered accountant, auditor, bookkeeper, payroll specialist, tax practitioner, financial controller and finance manager roles requiring finance credentials.
- Exclude HR Manager, recruiter, talent-acquisition specialist, labour-relations specialist and specialist HR roles.
- Exclude attorney, lawyer, paralegal and compliance roles requiring legal qualifications.
- Exclude medical, nursing, pharmacy, clinical and allied-health roles.
- Exclude civil, mechanical, electrical, mining and other professional engineering roles requiring formal registration.
- Exclude mechanic, electrician, plumber, driver, security guard, machine operator and other trades.
- Exclude procurement and supply-chain specialist roles unless the actual work is clearly transferable and no specialist background is mandatory.
- Financial adviser, insurance broker, investment, credit, property-agent and regulated gambling roles require verified licences, approvals and directly relevant evidence; otherwise exclude them.
- Treat donor fundraising, grant management and resource mobilisation as conditional. Apply only when the vacancy’s funding, compliance and sector requirements are demonstrably met.
- Exclude commission-only field sales, door-to-door schemes and roles whose real pay depends on recruiting others, even when the title says Business Development Manager.
- Exclude actuarial, advanced statistics, model-research and deep machine-learning roles requiring qualifications or tools not evidenced.
- Exclude cybersecurity, network engineering, DevOps and SRE roles when deep certifications, on-call operations or specialist experience are mandatory.
- Exclude MEAL, MERL and M&E specialist posts unless the role is genuinely centred on digital systems, communications, content or transferable project operations.
- Exclude roles with mandatory citizenship, language, clearance, professional registration or work authorisation that cannot be verified.

## 7. Vacancy decision process

### Stage A — hard-gate check

Reject or hold the vacancy when any of these fails:

1. Permitted geography and work arrangement
2. Genuine candidate-location eligibility
3. Legitimate paid employment
4. Mandatory professional licence, qualification, clearance or language
5. Feasible timezone, travel and physical-attendance requirements
6. Open and current application deadline
7. Verifiable employer and official application source

Do not treat preferred or “nice-to-have” requirements as hard gates.

### Stage B — evidence-based fit score

Score each vacancy out of 100:

| Factor | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Core duty overlap | 35 |
| Transferable systems, creative, project or commercial capability | 20 |
| Tools and technical-method overlap | 15 |
| Portfolio or project evidence | 10 |
| Seniority and responsibility fit | 10 |
| Remote, timezone and delivery fit | 5 |
| Trainability of remaining gaps | 5 |

Decision guide:

- **70–100:** Priority application
- **58–69:** Apply when the bridge is clear and no hard gate fails
- **45–57:** Apply selectively for junior, coordinator, support, generalist or urgent paid work when gaps are trainable
- **Below 45:** Do not submit unless there is an unusually strong, truthful reason

The exact title being absent from the CV must score zero negative points. Intentional downward seniority and overqualification alone must not reduce the score.

### Stage C — practical application rule

After all hard gates pass, use the score bands above. Apply only when Whitemore can credibly perform at least two of the vacancy’s three central duties, or an equivalent share when the vacancy has a different structure. Do not inflate fit by counting many minor or peripheral bullet points.

Also apply when:

- A preferred tool is missing but the underlying workflow is familiar
- He has more years of experience than requested
- The position is junior, associate, assistant or coordinator level
- The vacancy is paid contract, freelance, fixed-term, part-time or temporary
- The sector is new but the duties are transferable
- A degree is requested alongside “or equivalent experience”

Do not apply when a strict mandatory requirement is absent and cannot legally or practically be substituted.

## 8. Search operating procedure

### Freshness

1. Search the past 24 hours first.
2. Expand to the past 3 days.
3. Expand to the past 7 days.
4. Treat the past 10 days as the priority window, not a hard maximum.

## 10. Vacancy verification and scam gate

Before applying:

1. Confirm the employer exists and the vacancy appears on its official careers domain or a reputable ATS.
2. Confirm title, location, work arrangement, date, deadline, contract, pay basis and application URL.
3. Confirm the recruiter uses a credible company identity and domain.
4. Deduplicate the vacancy across platforms.
5. Never pay for an application, shortlisting, interview, placement, training, background check, visa, equipment or onboarding.
6. Never deposit a cheque, buy equipment for a recruiter, transfer money, use cryptocurrency or complete paid “tasks”.
7. Reject WhatsApp- or Telegram-only interviews, immediate offers without a proper process, vague duties, generic email addresses, pressure tactics and requests for banking or identity documents before verified onboarding.
8. Do not send passwords, full identity documents, bank details or sensitive records during an ordinary application.
9. Respect each platform’s terms, rate limits, account controls and anti-automation rules. Never bypass CAPTCHAs, access controls or security checks.
10. Do not sign declarations, consent statements, background-check permissions or legal attestations without verified answers and appropriate authority.
11. Avoid indiscriminate applications to many unrelated roles at the same employer; select the strongest credible matches.

Use the [FTC job-scam guide](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams), the [United Nations fraud alert](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/fraud-alert) and the [UNDP scam alert](https://www.undp.org/scam-alert) as baseline safeguards.
