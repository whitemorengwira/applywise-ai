# /job-matching — Three-Way Candidate Matching Skill

## Purpose
Performs holistic matching reasoning across:
**CANDIDATE EVIDENCE ↔ N.WHITE SYSTEMS PORTFOLIO ↔ TARGET JOB**

## Evaluation Model
1. **Deterministic Technical Overlap (40%)**: Compares verified technical competencies (AWS, Next.js 15, TypeScript, LangGraph, pgvector, etc.) against required skills.
2. **Architectural Seniority (30%)**: Verifies 14+ years of systems architecture and principal engineering leadership.
3. **Geographic & Work Arrangement Eligibility (15%)**: Evaluates South Africa, Zimbabwe, Malawi, or verified global terms via `EligibilityService`.
4. **Industry & Governance Alignment (15%)**: Assesses domain knowledge, regulatory compliance, zero-trust security, and immutable audit trails.
