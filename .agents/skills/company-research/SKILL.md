# /company-research — Company Intelligence Skill

## Purpose
Synthesizes company intelligence to ground cover letters, verify employer legitimacy, and prepare for interviews.

## Research Dimensions
1. **Corporate Identity & Industry**: Domain, founding, key executives, products.
2. **Technical Infrastructure & Stack**: Detected libraries, frameworks, cloud providers, and architecture patterns.
3. **Financial Health & Stage**: Funding rounds, runway, headcount growth trajectory.
4. **Cultural & Governance Focus**: Work mode flexibility, regulatory compliance, engineering standards.
